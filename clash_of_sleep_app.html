The Core Loop

One habit, gamified end to end.

Every screen exists to reinforce the same loop — sleep on time, prove you're awake, get rewarded, show it off.

‹
🌙

1. Set the alarm

Pick a school-day wake time. No snooze traps, no easy way out.

⚡

2. Prove you're awake

Math, a room photo, a tracked exercise, or a read-aloud check — before the alarm goes quiet.

🔥

3. Build the streak

Consistency compounds into XP and locks in King's Journey chest rewards.

🏆

4. Compete & customize

Spend points on your avatar, climb the leaderboard, repeat.

›
What's Inside

Every feature earns its spot in the loop.

🧠

Wake-Proof Challenges

Camera, mic, and step-based tasks scaled to your year level — math questions get harder as you do.

🔥

Streak Penalties, Not Resets

Breaking a streak costs points based on how long it ran — but your XP and level never go backward.

📦

King's Journey Chests

Day 3, 7, 14, 30, 60, 100 — bronze to legendary rewards that lock in permanently once earned.

🎨

Full Avatar Customization

Skins, borders, titles, backgrounds and effects — equip anything you've unlocked from one screen.

🏫

Global, School & Friend Boards

See where you rank against your grade, your school, or just your crew.

📍

Real Location & Time

Alarms respect your actual timezone, and photo checks are matched against real rooms in your house.
